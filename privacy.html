Optional analytics

Google Analytics is off until you accept. If you accept, Google receives technical information associated with your browser and requests, and the site records page views, selected publication, project, LinkedIn and contact interactions, and a section engagement event when at least half of a section heading is visible continuously for two seconds. These counts describe browsing activity. No visitor name or contact detail is attached to these events by the site.

The site sends fixed page addresses and named interactions rather than query strings, fragments, referral addresses, email-link destinations, or form values. Google Analytics may still process identifiers, device information, and network information associated with its service. Analytics is not configured here for advertising personalization or Google signals. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choice

Accept and Reject are equally available. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 180 days without renewal. You can change your choice using Privacy preferences. Rejecting after acceptance disables further site analytics, removes accessible Analytics cookies for this site, and reloads the page to unload the Google tag when the new choice can be saved. If saving fails, the site tries to clear the older choice and save a rejection for the current tab session. If neither is possible, analytics stays disabled on the current page without reloading. The notice explains the scope of the choice and any risk that an older saved choice may apply on a later visit. It does not erase information already received by Google. Browser settings may restrict saving choices or removing cookies.

Contact details and messages

The optional contact form sends your name, email, optional company, message, and any selected follow-up permission to Formspree to deliver the message to Kira. Submitting opens Formspree’s processing and confirmation page, which may include spam checks. See Formspree’s Privacy Policy. Kira uses these details to respond. The separate, unchecked future-opportunity permission is optional. Declining analytics does not prevent contacting Kira. Do not include sensitive personal, patient, medical, or identification information in the form.

Contact messages and identity details are not sent to Google Analytics. Message handling, storage, and deletion depend on the contact service and Kira’s mailbox. No fixed deletion schedule is promised here. You can contact Kira to ask about your message or withdraw future-opportunity permission.

Hosting, fonts, and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may process request information when you visit. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which makes requests to Google independently of your analytics choice. These services have their own privacy practices: GitHub privacy statement and Google Fonts privacy information. External links open third-party services, including Gmail, LinkedIn, publishers, GitHub, and Substack; their policies apply when you use them.
